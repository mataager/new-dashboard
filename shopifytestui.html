    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I set up my store for the first time?

    To set up your store for the first time, follow these steps:

    1. Complete your store profile in Settings > General
    2. Add your products in the Products section
    3. Configure payment methods in Settings > Payments
    4. Set up shipping options in Settings > Shipping
    5. Customize your store theme in Appearance
    How do I add my first product?

    To add your first product:

    1. Navigate to the Products section in the main menu
    2. Click the "Add Product" button
    3. Fill in product details like name, description, and price
    4. Upload product images
    5. Set inventory levels and variants if needed
    6. Click "Save" to publish your product
    Can I customize the look of my store?

    Yes, MATAGER offers several customization options:

    • Choose from multiple pre-designed themes
    • Customize colors, fonts, and layouts
    • Add your logo and branding elements
    • Create custom pages with our drag-and-drop editor

    Access these options in the Appearance section of your dashboard.

    Account & Billing

    How do I update my account information?

    To update your account information:

    1. Go to your Account Settings
    2. Click on the "Profile" tab
    3. Update your personal information
    4. Click "Save Changes" to apply updates
    How can I change my subscription plan?

    To change your subscription plan:

    1. Navigate to Billing in your account settings
    2. Select "Change Plan"
    3. Choose your new plan and review pricing
    4. Confirm the change

    Note: Plan changes take effect immediately, with prorated charges applied to your next billing cycle.
